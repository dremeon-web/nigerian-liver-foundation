Patients and families

A liver diagnosis raises many questions. Here is where to start, and how we can help.

If you have just tested positive for hepatitis

  1. Take a breath. A positive screening test is the start of finding out more, and many people with hepatitis live long, healthy lives.
  2. See a doctor soon. Further tests show how active the virus is and how your liver is doing.
  3. Ask the people close to you to get tested: your partner, children and others in your household.
  4. Protect your liver. Avoid alcohol, and do not take herbal mixtures or medicines your doctor has not approved.
  5. Keep your follow-up appointments, even when you feel well.

Questions to ask your doctor

  • What do my test results mean?
  • Do I need treatment now, or regular monitoring?
  • How often should I come back for check-ups?
  • Should I be checked for liver cancer, and how often?
  • Which medicines, supplements or herbs should I avoid?
  • Should my family be tested or vaccinated?

For families and caregivers

Hepatitis does not spread through sharing meals, plates, hugs or handshakes. Your relative needs your company, not distance. Get tested yourself, get vaccinated if you are negative, and help them keep their appointments.

How we can help

When someone needs specialist care, we refer them promptly to a tertiary hospital and follow up afterwards. If you or a relative need guidance, contact us and a member of our team will respond.
